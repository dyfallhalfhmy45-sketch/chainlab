Your next play starts here.

  1. Drag any player or the ball to reposition it. You can also edit a selected player’s coordinates.
  2. Choose a player and place the ball at their feet. Tap the pitch to set a target, then adjust speed, loft and spin.
  3. Kick to simulate. Pause, slow down or scrub the replay to study the ball’s flight.
  4. Switch between 2D and 3D anytime. Use Orbit, View angle and Zoom to change your perspective.
  5. Use Arrow to draw tactical runs. Save in this browser or export a JSON play to share and import later.

Influence zones show a fixed 7 m radius. Lane checks use a 2 m corridor; they do not predict interceptions. No real player likenesses or team branding are included.
